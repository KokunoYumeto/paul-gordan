{\large\bfseries T H E S E S. \textbullet}
\end{center}

\begin{enumerate}
\renewcommand{\labelenumi}{\Roman{enumi}.}
\item The division of functions, proposed by the illustrious Cournot into empirical and analytic functions, seems not to be suitable, because empirical functions are not given at all.
\item I contend that the method of infinitesimals is no less accurate than the method of limits.
\item It is of greater interest to inquire what qualities are inherent in a function defined by a differential equation than by what already known functions it can be expressed.
\item The principles of Democritus down to our own times are the foundations of the doctrine of atoms.
\end{enumerate}
