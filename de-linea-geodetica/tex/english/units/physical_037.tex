\sect{5}

We have now seen that the quantities
\[
 l,\quad B,\quad T,\quad s,\quad \psi,
\]
can always be computed from $x$, and therefore the whole of our inquiry will turn on the determination of this one quantity. We set down the following three especially important specimens of this fact.

I. Which point of the geodetic line has the latitudes $l$ and $B$? To compute $x$ from $l$ and $B$ we use the formula
\[
 {\cos B\over\sqrt{x'}\cos l}={1-2q\cos2x\over1+2q\cos2x}.
\]
Solving this equation, with regard to \gq{$2q\cos2x$}, gives:
\[
 2q\cos2x={\sqrt{x'}\cos l-\cos B\over\sqrt{x'}\cos l+\cos B}
\]
\[
\begin{aligned}
\cos2x&={1+\sqrt{x'}\over1-\sqrt{x'}}\cdot{\sqrt{x'}\cos l-\cos B\over\sqrt{x'}\cos l+\cos B}\\
&={\sqrt{x'}\{\cos l-\cos B\}+\{x'\cos l-\cos B\}
\over
\sqrt{x'}\{\cos l-\cos B\}-\{x'\cos l-\cos B\}}.
\end{aligned}
\]
\[
 {1-\cos2x\over2}={\cos B-x'\cos l\over \sqrt{x'}\{\cos l-\cos B\}-\{x'\cos l-\cos B\}}
\]
\[
 {1+\cos2x\over2}=\sqrt{x'}{\cos l-\cos B\over \sqrt{x'}\{\cos l-\cos B\}-\{x'\cos l-\cos B\}}
\]
\[
 \tg^2x={1\over\sqrt{x'}}{\cos B-x'\cos l\over\cos l-\cos B}.
\]
