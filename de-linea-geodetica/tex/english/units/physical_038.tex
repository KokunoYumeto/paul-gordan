Dividing the formula
\[
 s={2x\over\pi}\Sigma(l_0)+{4qb\over(1-2q)^3}\cdot{\sin2x\over1-2q\cos2x}
\]
by $\dfrac{2}{\pi}\Sigma(l_0)$ gives
\[
 x={\pi s\over2\Sigma}-{4q\over1+8q^2}\cdot{\sin2x\over1-2q\cos2x}.
\]
From this formula it is understood that $x$ differs only a little from ${\pi s\over2\Sigma}$; and, putting
\[
 x={\pi s\over2\Sigma}-4qv\cdot\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma},
\]
$v$ departs only a little from 1. Reducing this value in place of $x$ to our formula gives:
\[
 v\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}
=(1-8q^2){\sin\left({\pi s\over\Sigma}-8qv\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right)
\over
1-2q\cos\left({\pi s\over\Sigma}-8qv\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right)}
\]
\[
=(1-8q^2){\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}\left(1-32q^2v^2\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right)-\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}8qv\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}
\over
1-2q\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}+2q\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}\,8qv\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}}
\]
\[
 v\left\{1-2q\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}+16q^2v\cdot\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right\}
=\left\{1-8q^2\right\}\left\{1-32q^2v^2\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}-8qv\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right\}
\]
\[
 v=1-8q^2-16q^2v^2\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}-6qv\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}
\]
or, putting
\[
 v=1-6\omega q,
\]
\[
 6\omega=8q+6v\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}+16qv^2\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}
=8q+16q\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}+6\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}-36\omega q\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}
\]
and
\[
 6\omega=6\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}+q r,
\]
\[
 r=8+16\sin^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}-36\cos^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}=4\left(6-13\cos^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right).
\]
With these values reduced to $x$, it finally becomes, for the extremum,
\[
 x={\pi s\over2\Sigma}-4q\sin{\pi s\over\Sigma}\left\{1-6q\cos{\pi s\over\Sigma}-4q^2\left(6-13\cos^2{\pi s\over\Sigma}\right)\right\}.
\]
