\sect{3}

In order to compute the extension, the equation is to be transformed
\[
 s={2x\over\pi}\Sigma(l_0)+{b\over x'}Z{2Kx\over\pi}
 \qquad\text{Chap. VI \S\ 3.}
\]
With the formula
\[
 {2K\over\pi}Z{2Kx\over\pi}={4q\sin2x\over1-2q\cos2x}
\]
adjoined, it becomes:
\[
 s={2x\over\pi}\Sigma(l_0)+b{4q\over(1-2q)^3}\cdot{\sin2x\over1-2q\cos2x}
 \qquad\text{fund. p. 133.}
\]

\sect{4}

To compute the longitude, in the formula
\[
 \psi=-x{2K\over\pi}iZ(iv+K)+{i\over2}\log{H(iv+K)\over H(iv-K)}
 \qquad\text{Chap. VI \S\ 4.}
\]
the $H$ functions are to be expanded in series, and for $Z(iv+K)$ its value from Chap. VI \S\ 9 is to be inserted. Hence one obtains:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\psi+2\varepsilon{1-2q\over1-\varepsilon}x\cos A
&={i\over2}\log{\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)-q^2\sin3\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)
\over
\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}-x\right)-q^2\sin3\left({iv\pi\over2K}-x\right)}\\
&={i\over2}\log{\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)\over\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}-x\right)}
+{i\over2}\log{1-q^2\left[2\cos\left({iv\pi\over K}+2x\right)+1\right]
\over
1-q^2\left[2\cos\left({iv\pi\over K}-2x\right)+1\right]}.
\end{aligned}
\]
