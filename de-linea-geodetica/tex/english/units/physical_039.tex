\sect{1}

After the preceding arguments have set out the relations that hold between the variables of the defined geodetic line, in this last chapter we shall determine the elements themselves from certain given data.

Here let us confine ourselves to solving the question that first presents itself, namely:
\[
\text{\gq{What is the latitude $l_0$ of the circle touched by the shortest of all lines inside the points $A$}}
\]
\[
\text{\gq{($l_1,\varphi_1$) and $B$ ($l_2,\psi_2$)?}}
\]
This whole question is to be referred to computing $l_0$ from the formula
\[
 \psi_2-\psi_1=\psi=\int_{\varphi_1}^{\varphi_2} d\varphi\,\cos l_0
{\sqrt{1-e^2+e^2\sin^2 l_0\cos^2\varphi}\over1-\sin^2 l_0\cos^2\varphi}
\]
putting
\[
 \sin l_1=\sin l_0\cos\varphi_1,
 \qquad
 \sin l_2=\sin l_0\cos\varphi_2.
\]
