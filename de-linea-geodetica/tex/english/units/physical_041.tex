\]
We can reduce this equation to a more elegant form by using the well-known identity
\[
\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
1&\cos x&\cos y\\
\cos x&1&\cos(x-y)\\
\cos y&\cos(x-y)&1
\end{array}\right|
\]
in which, putting
\[
 x=\cot l_0\tg l_2,
 \qquad y=\cot l_0\tg l_1,
 \qquad x-y=\psi+\chi,
\]
one obtains
\[
\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
1&\tg l_2\cot l_0&\tg l_1\cot l_0\\
\tg l_2\cot l_0&1&\cos(\psi+\chi)\\
\tg l_1\cot l_0&\cos(\psi+\chi)&1
\end{array}\right|
=
\left|\begin{array}{ccc}
\tg^2l_0&\tg l_2&\tg l_1\\
\tg l_2&1&\cos(\psi+\chi)\\
\tg l_1&\cos(\psi+\chi)&1
\end{array}\right|=0,
\]
or:
\[
\text{I.}\qquad \tg^2l_0\sin^2(\psi+\chi)=\tg^2l_1+\tg^2l_2-2\tg l_1\tg l_2\cos(\psi+\chi).
\]
In this equation $l_0$ occurs both in \gq{$\tg^2l_0$} and in \gq{$\chi$}. But if for the moment we do not wish to define $l_0$ strictly, but rather a value $l_0'$ which is close to it, then $\chi$ may be neglected in the formula, since it contains the very small factor $e^2$.

Therefore $l_0'$, defined by the formula
\[
\text{II.}\qquad \tg^2l_0'\sin^2\psi=\tg^2l_1+\tg^2l_2-2\tg l_1\tg l_2\cos\psi,
\]
differs from $l_0$ by a quantity that, compared with the eccentricity, is of the second order.

To compute $l_0''$, which by its value approaches $l_0$ more closely, $\chi$ is not to be wholly thrown away, but account must be taken of the term
\[
 {e^2\over2}\cos l_0(\varphi_2-\varphi_1)
\]
of the series, progressing according to powers of the square $e^2$, into which the integral $\chi$ can be expanded. Then the value $l_0''$ defined from this equation,
\[
\text{III.}\quad \tg^2l_0''\sin^2\left[\psi+{e^2\over2}\cos l_0''(\varphi_2-\varphi_1)\right]
=\tg^2l_1+\tg^2l_2-2\tg l_1\tg l_2\cos\left[\psi+{e^2\over2}\cos l_0''(\varphi_2-\varphi_1)\right],
\]
recedes from $l_0$ by a quantity which, compared with the eccentricity, is of the fourth order.

At first sight one might think serious difficulties obstruct the computation of $l_0''$ from equation III, since $l_0''$ is contained not only in $\tg^2l_0$ but also in
\[
 {e^2\over2}\cos l_0''(\varphi_2-\varphi_1)
\]
