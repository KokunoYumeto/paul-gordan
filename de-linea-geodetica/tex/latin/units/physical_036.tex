\sect{4}

\[
 \psi=-x{2K\over\pi}iZ(iv+K)+{i\over2}\log{H(iv+K)\over H(iv-K)}
 \qquad\text{Cap. VI \S\ 4.}
\]
explicandae sunt $H$ functiones in series, et pro $Z(iv+K)$ ejus valor ponendus est ex Cap. VI \S\ 9.
Inde fit:
\[
\begin{aligned}
\psi+2\varepsilon{1-2q\over1-\varepsilon}x\cos A
&={i\over2}\log{\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)-q^2\sin3\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)

&={i\over2}\log{\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}+x\right)\over\sin\left({iv\pi\over2K}-x\right)}
+{i\over2}\log{1-q^2\left[2\cos\left({iv\pi\over K}+2x\right)+1\right]
\over
1-q^2\left[2\cos\left({iv\pi\over K}-2x\right)+1\right]}.
\end{aligned}
\]
