\begin{quote}
\gq{Linearum geodeticarum, eadem puncta extrema habentium, extensiones differunt quantitatibus minimis tertii gradus.}
\end{quote}

\begin{center}\S\ 12.\end{center}

Postquam igitur intelleximus, $\delta l$ convenienter definito, et linea geodetica usque ad terminum $O$ producta,
\[
 U=\int_{\psi_1}^{\psi_2}d\psi\{(11)\delta l'\delta l'+2(01)\delta l\delta l'+(00)\delta l\delta l\}=0
\]
fieri; demonstrandum erit, linea longius producta,
\[
 U<0
\]
existere posse.

Proinde bipartientes integrale nostrum ita, ut altera pars contineatur spatio a $\psi_1$ ad $\psi_2$, altera a $\psi_2$ ad $\psi$, posito:
\[
 \psi_1<\psi_2<\psi
\]
sumimus in prima parte:
\[
 \delta l=\alpha_1{dl\over d\psi_1}+\beta_1{dl\over dl_0},
\]
in posteriori:
\[
 \delta l=\alpha_2{dl\over d\psi_1}+\beta_2{dl\over dl_0}
\]
quibus in formulis
\[
 \alpha_1\qquad \alpha_2\qquad \beta_1\qquad \beta_2
\]
constantes sunt arbitrales. --- Inde fit:
