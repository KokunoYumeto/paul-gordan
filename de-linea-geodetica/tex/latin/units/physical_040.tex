Quamquam e posterioribus duabus aequationibus $\cos\varphi_1$ et $\cos\varphi_2$ determinari possunt, tamen propter periodicitatem functionum trigonometricarum ipsi anguli $\varphi_1$ et $\varphi_2$ determinabiles non sunt.

Jam igitur limites definiantur necesse est et qui continent $\varphi_1$ et qui $\varphi_2$. Quamobrem praesumimus (id, quod conceditur, quum omne punctum conversionis lineae geodeticae pro ejus exordio haberi possit) primum, intra exordium et punctum $A$ (sumto $l_2>l_1$) nullum interjectum esse punctum conversionis, tum intra puncta $A$ et $B$ non esse situm exordium.

Unde sequitur, ut (cf. Cap. II \S\ 8) $\varphi_1$ contineatur limitibus $0$ et $\pi$; ut $\varphi_2$, cum $\varphi_2-\varphi_1<\pi$ sit (cf. Cap. V \S\ 7) contineatur limitibus aut $\pi$ et $2\pi$, aut $0$ et $\pi$, prout punctum conversionis intra puncta $A$ et $B$ situm est, aut non est; hoc est, prout
\[
 \psi >\text{ aut }<\int_{l_1}^{l_2} dl\,{\cos l_0\over\cos l}{\sqrt{1-e^2\cos^2l}\over \sqrt{\sin^2l_0-\sin^2l}}
\]
est, quod integrale valore suo non longe recedit a summa
\[
 \arc(\cos=\tg l_1\cot l_0)-{e^2\over2}\cos l_0\,\arc\left(\cos={\sin l_1\over\sin l_0}\right).
\]
Sed ut revertamur, unde defleximus, $l_0$ computemus.
Posito:
\[
 \chi=\int_{\varphi_1}^{\varphi_2} d\varphi\,\cos l_0\,{1-\sqrt{1-e^2+e^2\sin^2l_0\cos^2\varphi}\over 1-\sin^2l_0\cos^2\varphi}
\]
aequatio nostra transit in:
\[
 \psi+\chi=\int_{\varphi_1}^{\varphi_2}{\cos l_0\,d\varphi\over1-\sin^2l_0\cos^2\varphi}
 =\arc\left(\tg={\tg\varphi_2\over\cos l_0}\right)-\arc\left(\tg={\tg\varphi_1\over\cos l_0}\right)
\]
vel, cum sit:
\[
 \arc(\tg=x)=\arc\left(\cos={1\over\sqrt{1+x^2}}\right)
\]
\[
 \psi+\chi=\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{\tg^2\varphi_2+\cos^2l_0}}\right)-\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{\tg^2\varphi_1+\cos^2l_0}}\right)
\]
vel, cum sit:
\[
 \arc(\tg=x)=\arc\left(\cos={1\over\sqrt{1+x^2}}\right)
\]
\[
 \psi+\chi=\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{\tg^2\varphi_2+\cos^2l_0}}\right)-\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{\tg^2\varphi_1+\cos^2l_0}}\right)
\]
\[
=\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{{1\over\cos^2\varphi_2}-\sin^2l_0}}\right)-\arc\left(\cos={\cos l_0\over\sqrt{{1\over\cos^2\varphi_1}-\sin^2l_0}}\right)
\]
